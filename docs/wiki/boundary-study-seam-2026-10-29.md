## 2026-10-29 — Boundary-Study Seam

On 2026-10-29, development selected a proposed `BoundaryStudy` module seam for
the first non-persistent visual-boundary experiment. The module would keep
world-space geometry, hit precedence, temporary movement, collapse projection,
and crossing-connection cues behind a small Interface in `crates/canvas`.

An `eframe` input and rendering adapter in `crates/app` would translate pointer
input and paint a renderer-independent frame. `crates/workspace-session` would
remain outside the experiment. A visual boundary, its temporary placements,
and its collapsed projection would not create a domain `Contains` or `Owns`
relationship, or a persisted Lens Layout.

The proposed seam required deterministic pure tests before a participant
session. Repeated study evidence would still be required before a durable
model or final interface decision.

Source: user conversation statement received 2026-10-29; [Core Interaction
Model](../product/core-interaction-model.md); [Boundary Discovery
Protocol](../product/boundary-discovery-protocol.md); codebase-design review
(2026-10-29).
