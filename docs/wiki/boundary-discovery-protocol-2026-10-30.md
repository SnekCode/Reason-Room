## 2026-10-30 — Evidence-First Boundary Discovery

On 2026-10-30, the product-reset continuation added a reusable boundary
discovery protocol for Phase 2 composition sessions. The protocol recorded
participant tasks, neutral prompts, observation fields, and decision gates for
testing how people interpret nested boundaries and crossing connections.

The protocol did not select a persisted boundary model, fixed organization
taxonomy, or final interface. It required repeated representative-session
evidence before a durable product or implementation decision.

## 2026-10-29 — Varied Temporary Study Examples

A participant noted that repeated use of one example can make the study feel
familiar before its visual language is understood. The temporary study now
offers two deterministic examples: **Account responsibility** and **Data
reporting**. Each has its own question, path, participating roles, affected
context, display categories, and color key.

The selector resets only temporary study state. It does not alter the
workspace, its facts, or the user's editing state. This change supports varied
observation. It does not select a final lens taxonomy or replace user
validation.

Source: participant feedback and temporary boundary-study implementation
(2026-10-29).
