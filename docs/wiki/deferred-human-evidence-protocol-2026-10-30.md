## 2026-10-30 — Deferred Participant Evidence Preparation

On 2026-10-30, the user directed continued autonomous roadmap work and deferred
manual testing. The project added a future-facilitator protocol at
`docs/product/deferred-human-validation-protocol.md`. It separates the five
roadmap discovery sessions from automated verification and requires a fresh
external workspace for each participant or retry through `./start.sh`.

The protocol records no session result. It does not mark a roadmap milestone,
semantic-zoom experience, boundary interpretation, or product-foundation
decision as complete. It requires later observations before a persisted
boundary, containment meaning, taxonomy, or other durable product commitment.
